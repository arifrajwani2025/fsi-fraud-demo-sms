Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Arif Rajwani operates the FSI Fraud Demo, a fictional, interactive demonstration. It is not a banking service and does not involve a real customer account.

Information collected and use

If you voluntarily join a live demonstration, the presenter enters the mobile number you provide into the Demo Control Center after obtaining your verbal consent. The system uses that number to send a simulated account activity SMS. If you reply, the system uses your response to illustrate case routing in the demo. The number and response may appear in the demo workflow and service logs. We use this information only to operate, troubleshoot, and demonstrate the requested interaction.

Service providers and sharing

The demonstration uses cloud workflow and messaging service providers, including AWS, Camunda, and Twilio, to process and deliver the demo message and response. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Choices and contact

You can decline participation and still watch the demonstration. Reply STOP to the demo number to opt out of further texts or HELP for messaging help. For questions about your demo information or a deletion request, contact: arif@rajwani.com.
